About Us

Local Crew, Straight Answers

Mr. Fix It KY started with one truck and a simple promise: show up, do it right, stand behind it.

Our Story

Built on Word of Mouth

Mr. Fix It KY has grown across Louisville and the surrounding Kentucky communities the old fashioned way, one finished job and one referral at a time. We are not a national franchise with a call center. When you reach out, you are talking to the people who will actually be at your door.

We keep the crew small on purpose so every job gets the same attention to detail, whether it is a single loose hinge or a full weekend punch list.
